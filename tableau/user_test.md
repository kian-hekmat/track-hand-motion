# First-time viewer test (required by the project spec)

Goal: find out whether someone who has never seen the project can read the dashboard without help.

## How to run it
1. Choose one person who has not seen this project and is not technical about computer vision.
2. Show them the dashboard (screen or the exported image) for about 10 seconds before saying anything. Do **not** explain it, name the project, or describe the pipeline.
3. Ask these questions in this order and write their answers **word for word** below. Do not correct or hint.
   - Q1. "In your own words, what do you think this shows?"
   - Q2. "What do the coloured bars represent?"
   - Q3. "What does the line underneath tell you? When is it high and when is it low?"
   - Q4. "Which recording looks different from the others, and how?"
   - Q5. "Is there anything you found confusing?"
4. Only after Q5, tell them the real story and note any surprises.

## What counts as understanding (pass criteria)
- Q1: says something like a hand/arm doing a repeated task, a sequence of steps, or movements over time.
- Q2: understands the colours are different stages or actions in time (they need not name all six).
- Q3: understands the line is movement or speed, high when moving and low when still.
- Q4: notices that Take 4 is much shorter/faster (and optionally Take 5 is messier).
If Q1, Q2 or Q3 fails, revise the dashboard (titles, labels, legend, caption) and test **another** new person.
